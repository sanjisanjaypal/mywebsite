About

Who I am, what I offer and how I work.

I listen first. Before I open a design tool, I want to sit with the people who will use the product and see how they work today.

I enjoy the problems many people find dull: dense data tables, multi-step approvals, compliance rules. Making those feel calm and obvious is the part of the job I like most.

Outside work, music and visual storytelling keep me curious. They remind me that design is as much about how something feels as how it works.
